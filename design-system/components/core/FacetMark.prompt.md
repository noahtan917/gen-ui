The Facet pair (upward "Build up" + downward "Cut down" triangles) in a tier or product color; use once per view to mark the thing that matters.

```jsx
<FacetMark tier="agentbase" size={16} />
<FacetMark tier="lex" size={24} />
<FacetMark tier="lift" size={40} /> {/* on Deep Blue */}
```

- Never rotate, flip, separate or outline it. Never use it as a bullet, arrow or play button.
- In cards it sits top-right of the single most important card.
